## Inspiration

NutRadar is an independently implemented Wi-Fi sensing dashboard inspired by [RuView](https://github.com/ruvnet/RuView). No RuView source code, trained models, datasets, or branding are included. The visual demo is synthetic and is not a reproduction of RuView's sensing pipeline.
